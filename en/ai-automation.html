Frequently asked
Will the AI assistant give my customers wrong information?
The assistant is set up to work only with information you approve. When it isn't sure, or a topic is outside its scope, it hands the request to your team. Every AI system still needs regular review, so we go over conversations together after launch.
Will it replace my team?
The goal is to free your team from repetitive work. Decisions and work that needs care stay with people; AI handles the first reply, information gathering and follow-up.
Which systems can it connect to?
WhatsApp Business API, Instagram, your website, email, Google Calendar, CRM software and most systems that offer an API. We review the tools you use during the discovery call.
